CPM Calculator

Calculate your estimated advertising revenue using CPM and impressions.








What is CPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) represents the amount advertisers pay for every 1,000 impressions. It is one of the most important metrics in online advertising.

How CPM Revenue Is Calculated

Revenue = (Impressions ÷ 1000) × CPM

Example

100,000 impressions with a $5 CPM would generate approximately $500 in revenue.
