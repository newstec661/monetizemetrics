What is RPM?

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) represents how much revenue you earn for every 1,000 impressions. Unlike CPM, RPM reflects your actual earnings.

RPM Formula

RPM = (Revenue ÷ Impressions) × 1000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good RPM?

A good RPM varies by niche, traffic quality and country.

Why is RPM different from CPM?

CPM measures advertiser cost while RPM measures publisher revenue.
