What is Affiliate Profit?

Affiliate profit is the money earned after subtracting advertising and operational costs from affiliate commissions.

Formula

Revenue = Sales × Commission

Profit = Revenue − Cost

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for any affiliate network?

Yes. It works for Digistore24, ClickBank, Amazon Associates and most affiliate programs.

Why should I track profit?

Revenue alone can be misleading. Profit shows whether your campaigns are truly generating money.
