ROI Calculator

Calculate your Return on Investment (ROI) and determine whether your investment is profitable.







What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) is a metric used to evaluate the profitability of an investment. It compares the profit generated with the amount invested.

ROI Formula

ROI = ((Revenue - Cost) ÷ Cost) × 100

Example

If you invest $500 and generate $1,000 in revenue, your ROI is 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROI?

Generally, a positive ROI indicates profitability. Higher ROI percentages indicate better returns.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the investment lost money.

Why is ROI important?

ROI helps businesses and marketers compare opportunities and make better financial decisions.
