What is YouTube RPM?

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) represents how much a creator earns for every 1,000 views after YouTube's revenue share.

Formula

Revenue = (Views ÷ 1000) × RPM

Example

100,000 views with a $4 RPM generates approximately $400 in revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RPM the same as CPM?

No. CPM is what advertisers pay. RPM is what creators actually earn.

Why do RPM values vary?

RPM depends on niche, audience location, watch time, seasonality and advertiser demand.

Can this calculator predict exact earnings?

No. It provides an estimate based on the RPM entered.
