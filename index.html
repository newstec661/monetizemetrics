Understanding Revenue Metrics

Online revenue depends on understanding the right metrics. Whether you're a content creator, affiliate marketer, advertiser or website owner, these metrics help you measure profitability and optimize your earnings.

What is CPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) represents the amount advertisers pay for every 1,000 impressions.

What is RPM?

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) measures the revenue earned for every 1,000 impressions.

What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) measures profitability by comparing profit and investment.
